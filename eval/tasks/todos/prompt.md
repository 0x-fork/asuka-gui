Make this app a todo list.

- The root component is `Todos`, and the `app` block shows it.
- A text field with the placeholder `New todo`, and a button titled `Add`
  that adds the field's text as a todo (not when it's empty) and empties
  the field.
- Each todo is a button: its title is the todo's text, or `Done: ` and
  the text once it's done. Tapping it marks it done, or not done again.
- A text `N left` says how many todos aren't done.
- A button titled `Clear done` removes the todos that are done.
