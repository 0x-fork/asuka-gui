Make this app a temperature converter.

- The root component is `Converter`, and the `app` block shows it.
- A text field with the placeholder `Celsius`.
- A text shows the temperature in Fahrenheit, `F: N`, for a whole number
  of degrees Celsius typed (F = C × 9 / 5 + 32, in whole degrees,
  rounded down): `F: 212` for `100`.
- While the field is empty or isn't a whole number, the text says
  `Enter a number`.
