Make this app a sign-up form.

- The root component is `Signup`, and the `app` block shows it.
- A text field with the placeholder `Email`, and one with the placeholder
  `Password`.
- A button titled `Sign up`, disabled until the email has an `@` with
  something on both sides of it and the password is at least 8
  characters long.
- Under the email, once something is typed that isn't such an email, a
  text says `Enter a valid email`.
- Tapping `Sign up` replaces the form with the text
  `Welcome, <email>!`.
