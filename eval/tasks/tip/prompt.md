Make this app a tip calculator.

- The root component is `Tips`, and the `app` block shows it.
- A text field with the placeholder `Bill`, for the bill in whole
  dollars.
- Three buttons titled `10%`, `15%` and `20%` choose the tip; 15% at
  first.
- Texts say `Tip: $N` and `Total: $N`, the tip rounded down to whole
  dollars; while the bill isn't a whole number, both are 0.
